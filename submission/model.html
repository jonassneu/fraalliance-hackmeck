How it works

Five public sources

  • Weather forecasts as issued ECMWF IFS + DWD ICON-D2
  • Published direction prognosis Umwelthaus INAA
  • Observed wind and gusts METAR EDDF
  • Official switching rules tailwind / crosswind limits
  • What was actually flown 11,564 hours

Calibrated logistic regression

  • Wind, gusts and the prognosis → direction flown
  • Refit on the latest 120 days follows the seasons
  • Calibrated on the last 14 days “80%” = right 8 in 10

48 h, hour by hour

  • Probability of 07 vs 25
  • Next switch with a time window
  • Runway 18 usable or not tailwind limit
