Official forecast = INAA (Umwelthaus). Backtest: walk-forward, each week is forecast by a model trained only on earlier data. Wind: ECMWF IFS, DWD ICON. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
Our forecast blends two models trained on the directions actually flown at Frankfurt over the last 120 days; one of them runs 200 wind scenarios around the ECMWF and ICON forecasts. Calibrated out of sample and tested hour by hour on Jun to Oct 2026. INAA is the official operating-direction forecast published by the Umwelthaus. The live record since 1 Nov is only a few days long, too short to judge yet.
